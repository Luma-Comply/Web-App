# Introducing Luma: AI-Powered Medical Necessity Documentation

We're thrilled to announce the launch of Luma. Our platform helps healthcare providers create compliant medical necessity documentation in seconds.

Gone are the days of spending hours on paperwork. Now you can focus on what truly matters—your patients.

## The Documentation Crisis in Healthcare

Healthcare providers face an overwhelming administrative burden today. According to the <a href="https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/prior-authorization/prior-authorization-survey" target="_blank" rel="noopener">American Medical Association</a>, physicians spend nearly two business days per week on prior authorization tasks alone.

That's time taken directly away from patient care. Furthermore, this administrative load contributes significantly to physician burnout across the industry.

### The Real Costs

The consequences extend far beyond lost time:

- **Treatment delays**: Patients wait weeks for approvals due to incomplete documentation
- **Revenue loss**: Insufficient documentation leads to denials and costly audit clawbacks
- **Provider burnout**: Administrative burden remains a leading cause of physician burnout
- **Patient outcomes**: Delayed care means delayed healing—or worse

## How Luma Transforms Your Workflow

Our AI-powered platform streamlines the entire documentation process. Meanwhile, it maintains strict HIPAA compliance throughout every step.

### Step 1: Enter Limited Clinical Context

Input only what's necessary for documentation generation. You'll provide patient first name, age, diagnosis codes, and treatment history.

No PHI required means you stay HIPAA compliant from day one. Additionally, our platform blocks common PHI patterns automatically.

### Step 2: AI Generates Documentation

Our AI researches current payer requirements in real-time. Then it generates comprehensive medical necessity documentation tailored to specific insurance criteria.

The system considers <a href="https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Medicare LCD/NCD requirements</a> and commercial payer guidelines. Consequently, your documentation addresses exactly what auditors look for.

### Step 3: Review and Export

Review the generated documentation with our built-in compliance checklist. Make any adjustments needed to match your practice's standards.

Export as Word or PDF when ready. Your documentation is prepared for immediate payer submission.

## Built for Compliance from Day One

We designed Luma with compliance at its foundation. Here's how we protect you and your patients:

- **Safe Harbor de-identification**: We collect only limited clinical context, never PHI
- **SOC 2 Type II certified**: Enterprise-grade security infrastructure protects your data
- **Zero data retention**: AI processing happens in real-time with no permanent storage
- **Audit-proof documentation**: Built-in compliance checklists ensure nothing gets missed
